Mouse & keyboard

  • Mouse steer (virtual stick) · X recentre
  • W / S throttle · Z match target speed
  • A / D strafe · R / F up / down · Q / E roll
  • Shift boost · Space slide
  • Left click guns · Right click / G missile
  • T nearest target · Tab next · C camera · Esc pause

Gamepad (Xbox / PlayStation)

  • Right stick pitch & yaw
  • Left stick roll (X) · throttle (Y)
  • RT / R2 guns · RB / R1 missile
  • LT / L2 slide · A / ✕ boost
  • Y / △ nearest target · LB / L1 next
  • D-pad strafe · L3 match speed · View / Share camera

Tip: hold slide, swing the nose round and shoot whatever is chasing you. Lure pursuers and missiles through the lattice towers.
